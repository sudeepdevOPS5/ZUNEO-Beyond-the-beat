# ✦ ZUNEO v1.0.0 Flagship Release ✦

Welcome to **ZUNEO v1.0.0** — **BEYOND THE BEAT.**

The next-generation open-source music streaming application for Android, featuring an **OPPO ColorOS Aquamorphic Design System**, high-fidelity streaming, synchronized multi-provider lyrics, offline caching, and Spotify sync.

---

### 🌟 What's New in Flagship v1.0.0

- 💧 **OPPO ColorOS Aquamorphic UI Transformation**:
  - Replaced legacy GPU-heavy glassmorphism blurs with solid, refined matte surfaces and subtle ambient shadows.
  - Native spring physics on all interactive buttons and cards with organic press feedback.
  - Generous ColorOS corner radii: 24dp on player artwork and standard cards, 28dp on hero modules and dialogs.

- 🎧 **Rock-Solid Background MediaSession Playback**:
  - Engineered with AndroidX Media3 for persistent foreground streaming.
  - Zero-lag playback when screen is locked or while multitasking in other apps.
  - Integrated lockscreen controls, notification drawer controls, and Bluetooth AVRCP support.

- 🌊 **Synchronized Live Waveform Seek Bar**:
  - Real-time scrolling waveform visualizer synchronized with track playback dynamics.
  - 4 Selectable Seekbar Styles: *Waveform*, *Wavy* (fluid sine wave), *Slim*, and *Default*.
  - Micro-tactile haptic ticks during scrubbing.

- 🎤 **Multi-Provider Synced Lyrics**:
  - Real-time word-by-word and line-by-line synchronized lyrics.
  - Automatic fallback engine querying LRCLIB, Kugou, and Musixmatch.

- 🎨 **Official ZUNEO Brand Palette & Presets**:
  - Signature dark OLED aesthetic: Deep Black `#080610`, Midnight Purple `#160B2B`, and Electric Violet `#7028FF`.
  - Curated themes including *ZUNEO Neon*, *Imperial Gold*, and custom swatch pickers.

- 📱 **Zero-Clip Adaptive Launcher Icons**:
  - Fully calibrated to Google's 72dp safe zone across all densities (`mdpi`, `hdpi`, `xhdpi`, `xxhdpi`, `xxxhdpi`).
  - High-resolution adaptive icon and Material You dynamic theming.

- 📥 **Universal Playlist & Album Importer**:
  - 1-Click public import from **Spotify**, **Apple Music**, and **YouTube Music** share links.

- 🔄 **Spotify Sync & ZUNEO Wrapped**:
  - Connect your Spotify account to mirror playback stats.
  - Spotify-style yearly wrapped summaries with top artist and song milestones.
